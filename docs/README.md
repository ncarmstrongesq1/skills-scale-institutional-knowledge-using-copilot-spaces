# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

OctoAcme manages projects through an iterative lifecycle. Initiation validates the business need, measurable outcomes, stakeholders, risks, and resources. Once approved, planning turns the initiative into a prioritized, estimated backlog with acceptance criteria, a Definition of Done, milestones, and identified dependencies. Teams execute in small increments, track work on a project board, and close sprints or milestones with demos and retrospectives.

Project Managers coordinate delivery plans, schedules, risks, and communication; Product Managers define outcomes, prioritize the backlog, and measure value. Developers build, test, document, and review changes, while QA validates acceptance criteria and quality. Stakeholders provide input and approvals, with Product Leads and sponsors participating in alignment and escalation.

The team coordinates through delivery standups, PM/Product Manager syncs, weekly delivery or stakeholder updates, and sprint or milestone reviews. Status updates communicate progress, next steps, risks, blockers, and decisions needed; blockers escalate from team triage through the PM and Product Lead to the sponsor when necessary.

Quality is integrated throughout delivery: new logic receives unit tests, integration tests are added where appropriate, and critical flows get end-to-end smoke tests. CI runs tests and linting, security scanning is expected, and manual QA is used when needed. Releases require acceptance criteria and CI/security checks to pass, with smoke tests and rollback or mitigation plans prepared; post-release verification and retrospectives help the team learn and improve.
